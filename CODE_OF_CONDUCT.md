# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction about impact.
- Respect privacy. Never publish another person's recordings, transcripts,
  identity, credentials, or private communications.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept moderation decisions and step away when asked.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained
disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable in project spaces or when publicly
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. If no private
moderation channel is listed, open a minimal issue requesting one without naming
people or describing sensitive details publicly. Maintainers will protect reporter
privacy as far as reasonably possible and may remove content, issue warnings,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards
consistently. This policy applies in repository spaces and public spaces where
someone is acting as a project representative.
